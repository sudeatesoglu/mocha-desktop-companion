# Getting started

Mocha lives in the corner of your screen on macOS.

1. [Download the DMG](https://github.com/sudeatesoglu/mocha-desktop-companion/releases/latest) for your Mac (`arm64` for Apple Silicon) and drag **Mocha** into **Applications**.
2. Open Mocha. This early version isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first time you need to allow it: **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.
3. Look for the top hat in your menu bar. That is where you see today's numbers, start a focus session, open the Tower and Settings, pause and quit.

## Around Mocha

- **Pet it** by moving your cursor onto it. Four quick clicks make it dizzy.
- **Drag it** anywhere; it remembers the spot. Clicks pass through everywhere except Mocha itself.
- **Double-click it**, or choose **Open Tower**, to visit its room.
- **Focus with Mocha** from the menu bar for a 25, 40 or 50-minute session: Mocha reads next to you, and a break starts when the time is up.
- **Settings** hold the lengths of work and breaks, quiet hours, opening at login, sounds and the browser extension.

Want Mocha to keep you company in the browser too? See [the browser extension](./browser-extension).
