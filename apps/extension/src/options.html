Mocha in your browser

Thanks for adding Mocha. Nothing is read until you turn it on in Mocha’s Settings.

Mocha keeps you company while you browse and talks about what you do, knowing only what you allow.

What Mocha may know

What is read
Only what you turn on in Mocha’s Settings, under Browser: the address and title of the tab in front, whether a tab plays sound, how many tabs are open, and, if you allow it, how often you type, scroll and click on a page. Never what you type or click on.
What is sent
Only to Mocha on this computer, and only what you allowed: the kind of site, its domain (like github.com), a title with addresses and numbers taken out, and counts.
What is never sent
Private windows; pages on this computer or your own network; banking and payments, health, government, mail and messaging, password managers and adult sites; sign-in, sign-up, checkout, payment and account pages; the sites you leave out below; anything while paused.
The sites that are never sent

    Addresses that look like banks, government, health care, mail or sign-in pages are held back too, even when they are not on these lists.

    What is kept
    Nothing about your browsing. Mocha keeps only the latest page in memory, to choose a pose and a comment, and soon forgets it. This extension keeps only the pairing code, the sites you leave out and whether it is paused.

    Pair with Mocha

    1. In Mocha, open Settings from the menu bar and turn on “Let the Mocha extension connect”.
    2. Copy the pairing code there and paste it here.

    What you allowed

    Change these in Mocha’s Settings, under Browser.

    Chrome needs your OK before the extension can see the tab in front. Chrome calls this “Read your browsing history”; your history itself is never read.

      Chrome’s permissions

      When you add the extension
      Nothing. It reaches only Mocha, on your own computer, and nothing else.
      “Read your browsing history”
      Asked here once you allow Mocha to know anything about your browsing. It lets the extension see the address and title of the tab in front. Your history itself is never read.
      “Read and change all your data on all websites”
      Asked only if you allow “Notice how busy you are on a page”. A small script then counts typing, scrolling and clicking on each page. It reads and changes nothing, and does not count keys in password fields.

      Sites to leave out

      Nothing is ever sent about these sites, or the sites under them.

        Pause

        Nothing is sent while paused.
